## 2. Regulatory Landscape — Implementation-Must

### 2.1 Biological Diversity Regime (Most Tested in SIH)
- **BD (Amendment) Act 2023 in force 01 Apr 2024** (notified 18 Jan 2024) [Conventus Oct 2024 report].
- **BD Rules 2024 notified 22 Oct 2024 (supersedes 2004), effective 25 Dec 2024** — simpler forms, drop-downs, online payments [Trilegal Oct 2024; Conventus Oct 2024].
  - **Form 1**: research / bio-survey / bio-utilisation (characterisation, inventorisation)
  - **Form 2**: commercial utilisation (drugs, enzymes, flavouring, cosmetics)
  - Fees: ₹10k individual / ₹20k entity (was flat ₹10k), quicker timelines.
  - **Foreign entity (Sec 3(2) person, incl. Indian entity controlled from outside) → NBA prior approval mandatory**.
- **NBA Annual Report 2024-2025 + New Benefit Sharing Regulations 29 Apr 2025** (with Central Govt approval) [Conventus Apr 2025]:
  - **DSI now explicitly within 'biological resource' for ABS** — each prescribed activity triggers benefit sharing.
  - **NBA approval required for commercialisation of IPR based on DSI accessed from India**.
  - **8 monetary + 14 non-monetary options (Form F)**, upfront payment for high economic value BR (red sanders, sandalwood, agarwood, threatened species Sec 38).
  - No distinction trader vs manufacturer for ABS calc.

> **Implementation trap for your wizard:** If query says “isolated DNA sequence from Tulsi” → must route to **BD Rules 2024 + DSI Regulations 29 Apr 2025 → NBA approval + Form 2 + ABS** — many teams will miss DSI=BR update.

### 2.2 Patent Regime (Ayush-Specific)
- **IP India Guidelines for Examination of Ayush Related Inventions — 2025** (Office of CGPDTM, DIPP, Ministry of Commerce) — 14 pages, url: `ipindia.gov.in/frontend/pdf/patents/guidelines/Guidelines%20for%20Examination%20of%20Ayush%20Related%20Inventions.pdf`
  - Scope: Ayurveda + Siddha + Unani + Sowa-Rigpa + Homeopathy + Yoga/Naturopathy, codified + non-codified.
  - **Guiding principle**: device/equipment for known Ayush procedure MAY be patentable if claims meet novelty/inventive step (e.g., “Advanced automated system for Vamana Karma with pH/temperature/weight/volume sensors + display + vomitus bag”).
  - Granted examples (proof it works):
    - 429737 (24 Apr 2023) Ayur.AI — integrative AI diagnosis
    - 377408 (21 Sep 2021) herbal vaginal douche
    - 416499 (02 Jan 2023) smart wearable for traditional medicine parameters
    - 535555 (29 Apr 2024) ICAR — herbal termite repellent for woody trees
  - **Brief on AYUSH-64** (CCRAS poly-herbal antimalarial, 1980, repurposed for COVID-19 via ICMR-NIN docking) — example of CCRAS patent via NRDC.

### 2.3 Global IP — GRATK

### 2.4 TKDL — Why You Must ABSTAIN
- **Access = 17 patent offices under TKDL Access (Non-disclosure) Agreement** (EPO, USPTO, JPO, UKIPO, CIPO, DPMA, IP Australia, IPO India, INAPI Chile, MyIPO Malaysia, Rospatent, INDECOPI Peru, OEPM Spain, DKPTO Denmark, INPI France, EAPO Eurasia, IPOPHL Philippines) — **no public access** [CSIR 2024 — tkdl.res.in/Abouttkdl.asp].
