# Team Thinking Patterns - Differentiation Intelligence
## How SIH winning teams actually think (vs masses)

### Pattern A: "The Crowded Default" (what 70% do)
> Pick PS that appears in `Top 10 Most Viewed` on sih.gov.in. Build ChatGPT wrapper + React frontend. Submit on deadline day. Get cut at internal or nationals.

**Markers:**
- Keywords in title: AI chatbot, smart app, portal, attendance, proctoring
- Org: AICTE, MoES, NTRO
- Tech: "Use React + Python + ChatGPT API"
- Pitch: "We help XYZ do ABC faster with AI"
- No comparison to existing solutions

### Pattern B: "The Strategic Picker" (top 10%)
> Pick PS by `acceptance potential × team fit`. Submit early. Add 1 measurable twist.

**Markers:**
- Filtered by org PS count (<10)
- Has screenshot of `X/500` counter showing low
- 1-week pre-research: read paper, find 3 competitors, contact org
- Mentions specific ministry KPI (₹ saved, beneficiaries)
- Demo link + GitHub link live at internal

### Pattern C: "The Differentiator" (top 3%)
> Pick domain-heavy PS others avoid. Solve the *real* pain judges know about. Build defensible moat.

**Markers:**
- Title has 2+ jargon terms (TKDL, GRATK, Section 3(p), Phytopharmaceutical)
- PS from misclassified theme (e.g., "Toys & Games" but actually LegalTech)
- **3 reasons judges remember them:**
  1. They cite a specific statute/article/rule in their pitch
  2. They correctly abstain when uncertain (cite legal "Information not advice" disclaimer)
  3. They show offline deployment, multilingual, accessibility — actual ministry deployment considerations
- They mention BOTH legal AND technical accuracy in their demo

### Winning Pitch Sentence Pattern
`"Unlike [Existing A/B/C], we use [Your Novel Method] to achieve [Measurable Benefit] for [Ministry KPI]."`
If you cannot fill this sentence — PS has no headroom, change PS.

### The "Silent Differentiators" Judges Notice
1. They know a 2024 amendment to a law that came out 3 months ago
2. They demo on a phone, not laptop (most teams demo on laptop)
3. They handle a query that should break their system — gracefully
4. They have a fallback plan if the API fails
5. They ship a working **deployment link**, not video

### Red Flags (Judge auto-reject signals)
- "We couldn't find a dataset so we used mock data" — without explaining fallback
- "We used ChatGPT to generate the entire code" — judges ask follow-ups and you blank
- "Our innovation is we used React" — that's not innovation
- "Our model is 99% accurate" — without test set
- No comparison to existing SaaS / startups / academic papers
